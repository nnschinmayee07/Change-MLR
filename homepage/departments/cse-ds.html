About the Department

From the HOD's Desk

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science) at MLRIT offers a specialized B.Tech programme focusing on data science, machine learning, big data analytics, and artificial intelligence. The department equips students with strong foundations in mathematics, statistics, and programming, combined with hands-on experience in modern data tools and platforms.

Vision and Mission

Vision

Achieve global recognition through innovation, interdisciplinary research and excellence in Data Science.

Mission

M1. Foster innovation and interdisciplinary research addressing real-world challenges using advanced data science techniques.

M2. Equip students with analytical, technical, and professional skills for industry excellence and entrepreneurship.

M3. Promote global collaboration with academia, industry, and research organizations ensuring ethical practice and societal impact.

Innovative Teaching Methodology

The department follows an active learning pedagogy integrating project-based learning, flipped classrooms, and industry-mentored sessions. Students engage in real-time data science projects, hackathons, and research activities. Regular guest lectures from industry professionals in AI, ML, and analytics complement the curriculum, bridging the gap between academia and industry.

Heritage of the Department ▼

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science) was established at MLRIT to meet the growing demand for data science professionals in industry and research. The programme is designed under the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) and follows an Outcome-Based Education (OBE) framework aligned with NBA accreditation standards.

The B.Tech (CSE-DS) programme prepares students for careers in data engineering, analytics, machine learning, and AI — domains that are at the forefront of technological advancement globally.

Departmental Academic Advisory Committee (DAAC)

A Department Advisory Board (DAB) is a structured committee that brings together faculty, industry experts and alumni to guide academic quality, align curriculum with industry needs, and support departmental growth. It acts as a bridge between academia and external stakeholders, ensuring programmes remain relevant and future-ready.

DAAC Responsibilities
  • Review and update curriculum and syllabus as per industry and academic standards.
  • Ensure alignment with Outcome-Based Education (OBE) — POs, PEOs, PSOs.
  • Guide academic planning and effective course delivery.
  • Promote innovative teaching-learning methodologies.
  • Strengthen industry interaction, MoUs, and internships.
  • Encourage research, publications, and innovation activities.
  • Monitor student performance and result analysis.
  • Collect and act on feedback from stakeholders (students, alumni, industry).
  • Recommend improvements in labs, infrastructure, and learning resources.
  • Support continuous improvement and accreditation (NBA/NAAC) compliance.
